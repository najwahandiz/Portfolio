Website built from scratch

AT Boost Website

I designed and built the AT Boost website from scratch, from its visual direction and responsive pages to its Laravel backend and protected forms.

From concept to website

I defined the visual direction, page structure, and user journeys, then built the frontend and backend. The public website introduces the AT Boost group and its relationship with Atirao. Visitors can explore agency expertise, service pages, resources, and routes for project enquiries and recruitment.

Laravel application structure

I created reusable Blade layouts and components to keep navigation, page structure, and common interface elements consistent. Laravel routes connect page and form requests to controllers. On the backend, I implemented validation and database storage for contact requests and job applications.

Form processing and spam protection

I integrated Google reCAPTCHA into the form flow and verified its response server-side. The Laravel backend validates submissions before storing accepted requests. This gives the site a defined path from the public form to the stored enquiry or application.

Measurement and technical SEO

I implemented tracking data layer events and variables for GTM and GA4, including separate signals for contact clicks and confirmed submissions. I also worked on Search Console, sitemap coverage, robots.txt, and indexing. For the local codebase, I added permanent redirects for legacy URLs and moved key service content into the initial Blade-rendered HTML.

Role
Full-stack developer
Context
Design and development
Technologies
PHP, Laravel, Blade, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Google reCAPTCHA

Atirao and AT Boost

SEO & Analytics Dashboard

A shared dashboard that turns analytics, search, and performance data for two websites into KPI cards, charts, and an action plan.

From measurement to reporting

I collaborated on a dashboard for Atirao and AT Boost. It draws on GA4 for site activity and tracked leads, Search Console for organic search visibility, and PageSpeed Insights for performance diagnostics. KPI cards and charts make those data sources easier to review together.

Keeping each website’s data separate

I worked on the site switcher and site-specific reporting. I connected AT Boost to Search Console and GA4, helped isolate its reporting data and caches, and handled empty GA4 responses so the dashboard could render even when a report returned no rows. I checked that the selected website displayed its own data.

Tracking across both websites

I implemented data layer events and variables for GTM and GA4. A click on a contact link and a successful form submission capture different actions, so I tracked them separately to give the reporting team a clearer view of confirmed leads.

Turning findings into an action plan

I used dashboard findings to help prioritize technical SEO, generative engine optimization (GEO), and performance actions. I worked on indexing checks, redirects, sitemaps, and the rendering of key pages. The dashboard provides the evidence for choosing what to investigate and what to check again after a change.

Role
Developer
Context
Collaborative project
Technologies
Next.js, React, TypeScript, MySQL, GA4 Data API, Google Search Console API, PageSpeed Insights API

Document-based AI assistant

Aura-Sadaqa

A community-support prototype that lets people ask questions about uploaded PDF and Excel documents.

Document upload and chat

I built a prototype that combines a document upload flow with a chat interface. Users can submit PDF and Excel files and ask questions about their contents in plain language.
